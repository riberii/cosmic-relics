01 • Problem & Vision

Turning "Space Hardware" Into Living Science Stories

🛰️ The Challenge Reality

Since the 1960s, NASA has deployed hundreds of rovers, orbiters, seismometers, and deep-space probes across the Solar System. When missions end, these machines remain behind on the Moon, on Mars, and in interstellar space.

School-age students often view them as static "space junk" rather than what they truly are: humanity's greatest scientific outposts and historical monuments.

🎯 Our Educational Mission

Cosmic Relics bridges the gap between hardware engineering and scientific discovery for youth.

  • Explain how the machines were engineered to survive extreme worlds.
  • Showcase what groundbreaking science they unlocked for humanity.
  • Empower students with interactive simulation, sound, and certification.

02 • Key Missions

Hardware on the Moon & Mars: Silent Sentinels

0 Watts

🌕 Apollo Retroreflectors

Hardware: 100 quartz corner-cube prisms placed by Apollo 11, 14, and 15 astronauts.

Science: Requiring 0 watts of power, they still reflect Earth lasers today to prove the Moon drifts 3.8 cm/year!

14.5 Yrs

🪐 Opportunity Rover

Hardware: Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT), solar panels cleaned by Martian wind devils, Rocker-Bogie wheels.

Science: Discovered hematite "blueberries" proving ancient Mars was drenched in liquid water.

72 Flights

🚁 Ingenuity Helicopter

Hardware: Carbon-fiber coaxial blades spinning at 2,400+ RPM in 1% atmosphere, smartphone chip.

Science: First powered atmospheric flight on another planet; scouted routes for Perseverance rover.

03 • Interstellar & Outer Planets

Voyager & Cassini: Beyond the Horizons

✨ Voyager 1 & 2: The Interstellar Voyagers

Status: Active in Interstellar Space • 15+ Billion Miles from Earth (49 Years in Flight!)

Hardware Architecture: Powered by Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTGs) converting plutonium decay heat into electricity. Equipped with the 12-inch gold-plated copper Golden Record.

Science: First man-made object to cross the heliopause into interstellar space, recording raw cosmic rays and interstellar magnetic fields.

🪐 Cassini-Huygens: The Noble Grand Finale

Fate: Deliberately plunged into Saturn's atmosphere on September 15, 2017

Planetary Protection: Cassini discovered water-ice geysers spraying from Enceladus's underground ocean.

The Sacrifice: To prevent any microscopic Earth microbe from surviving and contaminating Enceladus or Titan, NASA steered Cassini into Saturn's crushing clouds to vaporize safely.

04 • Project Solution

Cosmic Relics: The Interactive Web Application

🌌 Interactive Solar Explorer

Students filter relics by destination (Moon, Mars, Deep Space, Outer Worlds) and status (Active, Silent, Heroic). Dynamic starfield HUD provides real-time immersion.

🔍 Blueprint Inspection Modals

Interactive breakdowns of hardware components (RTGs, Rock Abrasion Tools, SEIS seismometers) paired with kid-friendly "Did You Know?" science discovery cards.

🔊 Web Audio Synthesizer

Synthesizes real radio carrier frequencies and telemetry beeps directly in the browser via the HTML5 Web Audio API—zero external audio dependencies.

05 • Data & Architecture

Grounded in Open NASA Scientific Data

🛰️ NASA Data Sources Utilized

  • NASA Planetary Data System (PDS): Instrument operational logs, Mössbauer & RAT spectrometry from Opportunity, SEIS telemetry from InSight.
  • NASA JPL Mission Archives: Voyager 1 & 2 interstellar trajectory, Cassini Grand Finale timeline, and Ingenuity flight logs.
  • Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (ALSJ): LRRR deployment coords and McDonald Observatory laser range verification data.

💻 Technology Stack

  • HTML5 & CSS3: Ultra-responsive, accessible glassmorphism interface styled with modern typography and fluid viewport scaling.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Lightweight state filtering, modal controller, and dynamic quiz assessment engine.
  • HTML5 Canvas & Web Audio API: Procedural starfield rendering and synthesized space telemetry tones.
