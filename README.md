## 🌟 Overview

Since the dawn of the space age in the 1960s, NASA has deployed rovers, seismometers, orbiters, and probes across our solar system. Today, many of these courageous machines remain on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. 

**Cosmic Relics** is an interactive, browser-native web platform designed specifically for school-age space enthusiasts (ages 8–15). It transforms these dormant machines from perceived "space junk" into living time capsules of human courage, engineering triumph, and scientific breakthrough.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

1. **🌌 Interactive Solar System Relic Explorer**
   - Seamlessly filter by cosmic destination (The Moon, Mars, Deep Space, Outer Giant Worlds).
   - Filter by mission operational status (Active & Transmitting, Silent Historical Monuments, and Planetary Protection Grand Finales).

2. **🔍 Interactive Hardware Blueprints**
   - Step inside the engineering behind iconic explorers:
     - **Apollo Laser Retroreflectors:** Zero-power quartz corner-cubes still measured with Earth lasers 50+ years later.
     - **Opportunity Rover ("Oppy"):** The 90-day martian rover that survived 14.5 years and discovered ancient liquid water evidence.
     - **Voyager 1 & 2:** Traveling 15+ billion miles in interstellar space, powered by Plutonium RTGs, carrying the Golden Record.
     - **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter:** Achieving powered flight in Mars's 1% atmosphere with carbon-fiber coaxial blades.
     - **InSight Mars Lander:** Listening to the heartbeat of Mars with ultra-sensitive SEIS seismometers.
     - **Cassini-Huygens:** The noble sacrifice into Saturn's atmosphere to protect alien oceans under planetary protection.

3. **🔊 Web Audio Telemetry Synthesizer**
   - Built with the HTML5 Web Audio API to procedurally synthesize deep space radio frequencies and mission carrier tones with zero external audio files.

4. **🎓 Cadet Mission Quiz & Printable Certificate**
   - A 5-question mission evaluation that tests student knowledge of hardware and planetary science.
   - Dynamically awards a personalized, printable **Certificate of Cosmic Exploration** that students and teachers can save as a PDF!

5. **📜 Planetary Protection & Space Archaeology**
   - Teaches students why space hardware is preserved as historic heritage sites and why spacecraft are safely vaporized to protect alien ocean worlds.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Zero-Dependency Architecture:** Standalone pure HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Web Audio API:** Real-time audio synthesis.
- **HTML5 Canvas:** Custom procedural starfield with dynamic twinkling.
- **Responsive & Accessible:** Glassmorphism UI calibrated for school Chromebooks, mobile devices, and desktops.
- **NASA Open Data:** Sourced directly from NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) and NASA JPL Mission Archives.
